SIGNAL PROCESSING AND COMMUNICATIONS REPORTS

Coursework reports by Eray Gundogdu at Bilkent University, covering signals and systems, digital signal processing and communications.

fourier-transforms-dft.pdf
Fourier-transform analysis and comparison of 28-point and 32-point DFTs.

least-squares-fourier-series.pdf
Least-squares regression and square-wave approximation with Fourier harmonics.

linear-phase-fir-filters.pdf
Low-pass, high-pass, band-pass and band-stop FIR filter design and audio tests.

gaussian-correlation-analysis.pdf
Bivariate Gaussian distributions, parameter estimation, correlation and orthogonality.

random-processes-spectra.pdf
Stationarity, autocorrelation, power spectral density and filtering of random processes.

uniform-lloyd-max-quantization.pdf
Uniform and Lloyd-Max quantization of a Laplacian source, evaluated through SQNR.

spectral-estimation-am.pdf
Random-process spectral estimation, moving-average filtering and DSB-SC modulation.

fsk-bpsk-detection.pdf
FSK and BPSK signal spaces, decision boundaries, error probabilities and MAP detection.

signal-generation-chirps.pdf
MATLAB signal generation, audio tones, phase and instantaneous frequency of chirps.

fourier-series-synthesis.pdf
Fourier-series synthesis and signal transformations through Fourier coefficients.

dtmf-audio-analysis.pdf
DTMF transmission and decoding using Fourier analysis, with recorded-audio experiments.

image-convolution-filtering.pdf
2D convolution for image smoothing, edge detection and template-based pattern recognition.

sampling-interpolation.pdf
Signal sampling and reconstruction using zero-order hold, linear and sinc interpolation.

discrete-time-lti-systems.pdf
Difference-equation implementation, impulse responses and frequency-response analysis.
